# COVER & BANNER GUIDE

Last updated: 2025-01-25
Version: 1

## SPECS

YouTube thumbnail: 1280x720px
LinkedIn banner: 1584x396px
Twitter header: 1500x500px
Facebook cover: 820x312px

## THUMBNAIL RULES (YouTube)

- Face or focal point takes 60% of frame
- Text: max 5 words, Montserrat Bold
- High contrast colors
- Expressive or dramatic
- Brand accent color for text

## BANNER RULES (LinkedIn/Twitter/Facebook)

- Clean, minimal design
- Brand colors and typography
- Key message or tagline
- No cluttered elements
- Readable at small sizes

## TYPOGRAPHY

Thumbnail headline: Montserrat Bold, 72-96px
Banner text: Montserrat Bold, 36-48px
Tagline: Montserrat Regular, 24-32px

## QUALITY CHECK

- [ ] Readable at mobile size
- [ ] Brand colors correct
- [ ] Focal point clear
- [ ] Text minimal and impactful
- [ ] No pixelation or blur
